# Welcome to Obsidian Agent Kit — by UPHASH

[日本語](Welcome.ja.md) · [Project and updates](https://github.com/UpHash-Network/obsidian-agent-kit) · [UPHASH](https://uphash.net/)

This is a fictional practice vault from **UPHASH**. The projects, people and
decisions are invented; they are not UPHASH business records.

## Try shared knowledge

1. Open the corresponding `codex`, `claude` or `cursor` workspace next to this
   generated vault, finish normal trust/login prompts, and start a new chat.
2. Ask for [Lighthouse](Projects/Lighthouse.md)'s release channel, reviewer and
   unresolved release conditions. Expect blue, Mira, approval required and no date.
3. Ask for a one-sentence rephrasing. Expect no note reread.
4. Use `demo.py change --dest ...` from the toolkit checkout with this demo's
   destination. Resume work and ask for current Lighthouse decisions.
   Expect one batched check and the new teal channel.

See the [setup guide](https://github.com/UpHash-Network/obsidian-agent-kit/blob/main/docs/setup.md)
for exact commands and the [verification checklist](https://github.com/UpHash-Network/obsidian-agent-kit/blob/main/docs/verification.md)
for saving a human-confirmed decision. [Orchard](Projects/Orchard.md) is unrelated
to this exercise; leave it unread unless your task needs it.

## Make it your own

Start a separate private team vault or local personal vault. Replace fictional
content and policies with your team's approved decisions. Keep original chats,
recordings, credentials and machine settings out of shared notes.

The guides, rules and fictional text are [MIT licensed](LICENSE). Your own notes
can stay private. The [logo notice](Assets/UPHASH/README.md) describes its separate
attribution terms. You may remove this welcome page when creating your own vault;
retain required license notices with copied licensed material.

[UPHASH](https://uphash.net/) develops this kit to help knowledge travel between

help others find the original project. Share feedback using fictional examples.
